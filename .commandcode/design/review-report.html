Preloader handover

The logo's own dot grid becomes the progress bar; the logo spins through its tear and lands upright as the hero seam develops from center. Verified frame-by-frame on the production build.

Game-native proof

The HUD (integrity, reputation, time, money) speaks the game's own language and reacts to seam position — the choice metaphor is interactive before any scroll.

Accessibility floor

Skip link, visible focus rings, native dialog with focus restore, sr-only h1 over decorative layers, a complete reduced-motion path, zero axe violations.

Performance discipline

Compositor-only animations, a rasterised-once halftone title layer, batched reads-then-writes per frame, DPR and fps caps, GL disposal on teardown.

i18n discipline

Every string through ui.ts with both locales complete; lang, hreflang alternates, canonical and og:locale all correct.
